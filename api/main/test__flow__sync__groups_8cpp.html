"Synchronization group : Repeated waits"  ,
""  [mxl sync groups] 
)

A synchronization group must keep synchronizing for as long as it exists.

FlowSynchronizationGroup::waitForDataAt opportunistically moves the flow with the largest observed source delay to the front of its reader list. This test makes the second member the slower one, so that the reordering is triggered, and then checks that the group still waits on its members afterwards.
